# «Funded by the European Union» — the official files

Downloaded on 20 November 2026 from the European Commission's logo download
centre, <https://ec.europa.eu/regional_policy/information-sources/logo-download-center_en>
(`funded_en.zip`, `funded_it.zip`), horizontal version, RGB PNG:

| here | official file |
|---|---|
| `funded-by-eu-pos.png` | `EN_FundedbytheEU_RGB_POS.png` — for a light ground |
| `funded-by-eu-neg.png` | `EN_FundedbytheEU_RGB_NEG.png` — for a dark ground (white words, white edge round the flag) |
| `it/funded-by-eu-pos.png` | `IT_FundedbytheEU_RGB_POS.png` |
| `it/funded-by-eu-neg.png` | `IT_FundedbytheEU_RGB_NEG.png` |

Only scaled (sips -Z 900, from 4125 px), never redrawn, recoloured or cut: the
emblem and its statement stay one image. The rules followed: the emblem with
the words «Funded by the European Union», unaltered; shown next to other logos,
at least as prominently and visibly as they are (Horizon Europe grant
agreement, art. 17 and its annex on visibility); the positive file on a light
ground, the negative one (white edge, white words) on a dark ground; never
smaller than the minimum of the EU visual identity (flag 1 cm high, about
38 px on screen). The manuals show it at the height of the other logos or
higher, and with the disclaimer of Horizon Europe beside it.

Other languages: the same zip exists per official language
(`funded_<lang>.zip`); a manual in a language without its own copy here shows
the English one.
